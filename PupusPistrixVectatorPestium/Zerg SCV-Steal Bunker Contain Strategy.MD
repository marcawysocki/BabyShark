Zerg SCV-Steal Bunker Contain Strategy

This document summarizes the complete worst-case tech requirements and tactical sequence for executing a Zerg strategy that:

Maintains a Terran bunker at 1 HP

Times Neural Parasite to steal the repairing SCV

Pops the bunker and wipes the Marines

Extracts the stolen SCV to build a Terran Command Center

1. Core Concept

A Terran bot performing a bunker contain will keep an SCV repairing the bunker. By keeping the bunker at 1 HP, the SCV becomes permanently pinned. Zerg can then:

Burrow Infestors

Approach unseen

Cast Neural Parasite on the repairing SCV

Kill the bunker and Marines

Walk the stolen SCV away to build Terran structures

This strategy is upgrade-proof and works even if Terran is researching every possible upgrade.

2. Required Zerg Tech (Worst Case)

To guarantee success regardless of Terran upgrades, Zerg must research:

Mandatory Tech

Lair – unlocks all required tech

Roach Warren – enables Roaches and Ravagers

Ravager Morph – provides Corrosive Bile for Marine one-shots

Burrow – allows Infestors to approach undetected

Infestation Pit – required for Infestors and Neural Parasite

Neural Parasite – required to steal the repairing SCV

Recommended Tech

Pathogen Glands – Infestors spawn with 100 energy, enabling multiple Neurals if Terran pulls extra SCVs

3. Key Unit and Ability Mechanics

Bunker

HP: 400

Burns below 50% HP

Repairing stops burn

Keeping it at 1 HP forces the SCV to repair continuously

Infestors

Start with 75 energy (100 with Pathogen Glands)

Regenerate 0.5625 energy/sec

Can cast Neural Parasite while burrowed

Neural Parasite range: 9

Corrosive Bile (Ravager)

Damage: 60

One-shots Marines (45 HP) even with Combat Shield (55 HP)

Total delay from cast to impact: ~1.96 seconds

Perfect for pre-casting to land as the bunker dies

4. Combat Sequence

Phase 1 — Bunker Control

Use Roaches to keep the bunker at 1 HP

Avoid killing the repairing SCV

Maintain vision and pressure

Phase 2 — Infestor Setup

Burrow Infestors

Move into position within 9 range of the repairing SCV

Phase 3 — Neural Execution

Cast Neural Parasite on the repairing SCV

Immediately issue:

Kill bunker (1 volley)

Kill all Terran units except the Neural’d SCV

Phase 4 — Marine Wipe

Pre-cast Corrosive Bile ~2 seconds before bunker death

Bile lands as Marines exit

All Marines die instantly

Phase 5 — Extraction

Move stolen SCV to a hidden location

Build a Command Center or any Terran tech

Cancel Neural if desired

5. Why This Works Against Fully Upgrading Terran

Even if Terran upgrades everything (Stim, Combat Shield, Infantry Weapons/Armor, etc.), the strategy remains reliable because:

Corrosive Bile ignores armor and upgrades

Neural Parasite ignores upgrades

Bunker HP is fixed

Marine HP upgrades do not prevent bile one-shots

Burrowed Infestors bypass all Terran DPS

6. Summary

This strategy is a robust, upgrade-proof method for stealing a Terran SCV during a bunker contain. By combining precise bunker HP control, burrowed Infestor positioning, Neural Parasite timing, and Corrosive Bile synchronization, Zerg can reliably:

Break the contain

Eliminate the Marines

Capture an SCV

Build Terran structures for strategic or comedic advantage
